# Code of Conduct

Memstead is a software project. This code covers how people treat each other
in its spaces: the repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and any
channel the project runs. It applies to everyone in the same way.

## Expected

- Keep to the subject: the engine, its design, its use.
- Criticise work, not people. Disagree with reasons.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks, towards anyone and on any
  ground.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption: off-topic flooding, deliberate provocation.
- Political, religious or ideological debate. These are left out of the
  project's spaces whatever their direction, so the work stays open to
  everyone regardless of where they stand.

## Reporting

Write to **hello@memstead.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept
confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove contributions that break this code,
and may restrict or end someone's participation. Decisions are proportionate
to what happened, and the reason is stated where that is possible.
